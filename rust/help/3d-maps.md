# 3D maps

Choose **3D map** above the engine, request or ETV table. The coloured surface joins the map's actual points.

| Axis | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **RPM** | Engine speed. |
| **Throttle (%)** or **Pedal (%)** | Throttle opening for the engine map; pedal position for request and ETV maps. |
| **Nm** or **% throttle** | Height: torque for engine/request maps; throttle target for ETV maps. |

## Inspect the surface

1. Drag the surface to rotate it. Scroll over it to zoom.
2. Hover near a point to read its RPM, position and value.
3. Click **Reset view** to restore the starting angle and zoom.

**Rotation**, **Tilt** and **Zoom** also work with the keyboard. Choose **Table** to edit values; viewing the surface does not change the map.

Colours use the table's scale. ETV maps keep 0–100%; torque maps use their own range. Uneven breakpoint spacing is preserved. A single row or column appears as a line; one cell appears as a point.
