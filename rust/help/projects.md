# Projects and appearance

## Keep your work

| Do this | Get that |
|---|---|
| **Save as…** | Save inputs, settings and result together in a new .openetv project. |
| **Save** or Command-S | Update the current project. |
| **Open project…** | Continue a saved project. |

If another application changed the file, **Save** refuses to overwrite it. Use **Save as…** to keep a separate copy. A restored result keeps corrections only when its saved calculation matches its inputs.

## Appearance

Open **Appearance** to choose the theme, font and interface size. Click **Save appearance** to keep the choice.

Dark and Light use your accent colour. Industrial and Terminal keep their own signal colours. Map colours describe values and stay the same across themes.
